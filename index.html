About

I'm a freelance Minecraft Bedrock developer. I've built 10+ add-ons and contributed to 30+ maps released on the official Minecraft Marketplace, working remotely with partner studios on spell and ability systems, combat classes, pets, custom weapons, bosses and camera tools.

I care about code that holds up: an event-driven architecture, performance-aware scripting and modules that stay easy to maintain and hand over to another developer.

Alongside this I'm studying for an engineering degree in computer science at Polytech Nice-Sophia and working as a cybersecurity apprentice at SAP Labs France.
